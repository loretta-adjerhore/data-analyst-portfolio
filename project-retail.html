Choco de Luxe | European Sales Performance Analytics

Multi-market analysis of €5.32M in luxury chocolate sales across 7 European cities, uncovering a €672K revenue recovery opportunity, channel profitability gaps, and a sales force optimisation framework worth €500K+ annually.

Choco de Luxe Location Performance Dashboard — Geographic revenue distribution, delivery success metrics, and city-level strategic insights across 7 European markets

Tableau SQL Excel E-commerce Analytics Multi-Market Analysis

1. The Business Problem

Choco de Luxe is a premium artisanal chocolate brand headquartered in Brussels, Belgium, operating across 7 European cities with multi-channel distribution spanning website, warehouse, and social media. Despite a strong brand presence and €5.32M in annual revenue, leadership was making expansion and investment decisions without any data-driven foundation.

The business had no unified visibility into which cities, channels, or products were truly driving profitability. Some locations were consistently underperforming with unclear root causes, and the sales team of 25 representatives showed wide performance variance with no framework to understand why top performers succeeded or how to replicate their results.

The core challenges: Leadership could not identify which of their 7 cities, 3 sales channels, or 21 product SKUs were genuinely profitable versus consuming resources without return. Delivery performance was inconsistent across locations with no visibility into root causes. And with 56% of the sales team missing their €100K profit target, there was no data foundation for territory optimisation, coaching, or performance management — leaving significant revenue on the table every quarter.

2. Key Performance Metrics

€5.32M Total Revenue
€2.45M Total Profit
46% Profit Margin
177,007 Boxes Shipped
95.61% Delivery Success Rate
1,094 Total Orders Across 7 Cities

The 46% profit margin demonstrates strong premium positioning in the luxury chocolate segment. However, with only 11 of 25 sales representatives meeting their €100K profit target and a €672K revenue gap between the top and bottom performing cities, significant optimisation opportunity exists — without requiring any new market entry or product development.

3. Analytical Methodology

01

Multi-Market Geographic Analysis

Compared performance across all 7 European cities — Turin, Barcelona, Amsterdam, Zurich, Brussels, Hamburg, and Lyon — across revenue, profit, delivery success rate, and total order volume. Identified top and bottom performers and investigated the root causes behind performance gaps to distinguish operational issues from market penetration issues.

02

Channel Profitability Study

Conducted a detailed profitability analysis across the three distribution channels — Website, Warehouse, and Social Media — comparing revenue contribution, profit margins, and customer acquisition dynamics. Assessed product-channel alignment to identify where strategic investment would generate the highest ROI.

03

Tableau Dashboard Development

Built a 3-page interactive Tableau dashboard suite: Page 1 covering location and sales performance with city comparison and delivery metrics; Page 2 covering product and channel profitability; Page 3 covering individual sales team performance against the €100K profit target. All pages include interactive filtering and drill-down capabilities across multiple dimensions.

04

Sales Force Evaluation & Recommendations

Tracked individual performance for all 25 sales representatives against the €100K profit target. Identified top and bottom performers, analysed the 2.2x performance spread between best and worst, and developed a data-driven framework for territory optimisation, mentorship, and incentive alignment to close the performance gap across the team.

4. Key Insights

Insight 1 — €672K Revenue Gap Between Cities Reveals Untapped Potential

The top 3 cities generate 61% of total revenue: Turin, Italy (€1,026,368 | 97.07% delivery success), Barcelona, Spain (€1,004,109 | 95.63%), and Amsterdam, Netherlands (€980,001 | 94.61%). Meanwhile Lyon, France — the bottom performer — generates only €358,260 despite achieving a perfect 100% delivery success rate. This is the critical finding: Lyon's operational execution is flawless, but revenue is 65% below Turin. This means the problem is market penetration, pricing, or product mix — not operations. If Lyon reaches just 75% of Turin's performance, that represents €300K+ in annual revenue recovery.

Location Performance Dashboard — Revenue by city showing Turin (€1.03M) as top performer and Lyon (€358K) as the highest-opportunity underperformer despite perfect delivery

Insight 2 — Website Channel Delivers 62% Higher Margins Than Social Media

Channel profitability analysis revealed a dramatic margin gap across the three distribution channels. The Website channel generates €2.43M revenue at a 45.8% profit margin — the highest of all three channels. Warehouse contributes €1.51M at 28.3% margin, while Social Media generates €1.38M at only 25.9% margin. The website's direct-to-consumer model supports premium pricing, with organic products representing 45% of website sales. The strategic implication is clear: the website delivers 62% higher margins than social media despite lower absolute volume. Reallocating 15% of the marketing budget from lower-margin channels to website optimisation is projected to deliver €250K+ annual profit improvement.

Channel Profitability Analysis — Website (45.8% margin) vs Warehouse (28.3%) vs Social Media (25.9%) demonstrating the strategic case for channel investment rebalancing

Insight 3 — 56% of Sales Team Missing Target With Replicable Success Patterns

Performance tracking against the €100K profit target revealed a significant team-wide gap: only 11 of 25 representatives are meeting target (44% success rate), while 14 are underperforming. The performance spread is 2.2x between the top performer Karlen McCaffrey (€131,994 — 32% above target) and the bottom performer Wilone O'Kielt (€54,503 — 45% below target). Critically, this wide variance suggests territory assignment, product knowledge, and training gaps — not fundamental market limitations. The success patterns of top performers are identifiable and replicable through data-driven coaching and territory optimisation.

Sales Team Performance Dashboard — Individual tracking against €100K profit target showing 11 of 25 representatives meeting target with a 2.2x performance spread

6. Strategic Recommendations

Recommendation 1 — Lyon Turnaround Initiative (Urgent — €300K+ Opportunity)

Conduct an immediate root cause analysis in Lyon focused on market penetration, product-market fit, and pricing strategy. Deploy a top-performing sales representative from the Turin market to share best practices and drive account development. Reassess the product mix offered in Lyon against local customer preferences. Target: bring Lyon to 75% of Turin's performance level within 6–12 months, representing €300K+ in annual revenue recovery from a market that already has perfect operational delivery.

Recommendation 2 — Channel Investment Rebalancing (€250K+ Margin Improvement)

Reallocate 15% of warehouse and social media marketing budgets to website channel optimisation. Enhance website functionality, user experience, and conversion optimisation — particularly for the organic product range which represents 45% of website sales. The website's 45.8% margin versus social media's 25.9% makes this the highest-ROI reallocation available without any new product or market investment. Projected 10–12% overall margin improvement representing €250K+ annual profit increase.

Recommendation 3 — Delivery Performance Optimisation (€90K Value Protection)

Hamburg and Brussels are both underperforming at 92.70% delivery success — significantly below the 97%+ benchmark achieved by Turin and Zurich. Investigate logistics bottlenecks in these two markets, implement real-time delivery tracking, and review last-mile delivery partner performance. Target: achieve 97%+ delivery success across all 7 markets, protecting customer satisfaction and repeat purchase rates in these key revenue markets.

Recommendation 4 — Sales Force Development (€150K+ Profit Improvement)

Implement a structured mentorship programme pairing top performers (Karlen McCaffrey, Beverie Moffet, Kelci Walkden) with underperforming representatives. Conduct territory optimisation analysis to ensure fair market coverage. Introduce performance-based incentive structures and quarterly product knowledge training. The goal: move all 25 representatives above the €100K profit threshold, closing the current 2.2x performance gap through replicable, data-backed coaching frameworks.
