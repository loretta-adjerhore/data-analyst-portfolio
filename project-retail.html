Choco de Luxe
European Retail Performance Analytics

Uncovering why sales were falling short of expectations and building a strategy to close the gap across European markets.

€672K Revenue Gap Tableau Europe · Retail 2024

The Brief

Choco de Luxe is a premium European confectionery brand selling across multiple markets. The business had been growing, but their actual sales figures were consistently falling below what they expected to be making. No one had a clear picture of why — or where exactly the gap was.

The task was to dig into the sales data, understand which markets, products, and time periods were underperforming, and put together a clear optimisation strategy that the business could act on.

What I Did

Step 01

Mapped Sales Across Markets

Looked at how each European market was performing and compared them against each other to identify where the biggest gaps were sitting.

Step 02

Analysed Seasonal Trends

Broke down sales by month and season to understand when the business was losing ground and whether there were predictable patterns to work with.

Step 03

Reviewed the Product Mix

Examined which product lines were driving revenue and which ones were dragging performance — looking at both volume and margin contribution.

Step 04

Built the Dashboard

Created an interactive Tableau dashboard showing the full sales picture, with filters for market, product, and time period so the team could explore the data themselves.

Key Findings

  • The revenue gap was concentrated in specific markets A small number of markets were responsible for the majority of the shortfall, making it easier to prioritise where to focus first.
  • Seasonal dips were happening at predictable times but had no mitigation in place The business was losing revenue during known slow periods without any promotional or stock strategy to offset the drop.
  • A small number of products were driving most of the revenue The product mix was unbalanced — bestsellers were not being pushed hard enough while weaker lines were taking up shelf space and marketing budget.
  • Adjusting the product and market strategy could recover a significant portion of lost revenue By focusing on the right products in the right markets at the right times of year, the business had a clear path to closing the performance gap.
