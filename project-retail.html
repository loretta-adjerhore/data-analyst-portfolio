Choco de Luxe
European Sales Performance

Analysing €5.32M in luxury chocolate sales across 7 European cities to identify growth opportunities and optimise performance.

€5.32M Revenue Tableau 7 Cities 2024

The Brief

Choco de Luxe is a premium artisanal chocolate brand operating across 7 European cities through retail, online, and supermarket channels. Despite strong brand recognition, leadership was making expansion decisions without a clear picture of which cities, channels, or sales representatives were actually driving profitability.

The project involved analysing 1,094 orders across 21 product SKUs and 25 sales reps to deliver a complete view of performance — and a roadmap for where to invest next.

What I Did

Step 01

Geographic Analysis

Compared performance across all 7 cities, evaluating revenue, delivery success, order volume, and profit margins to rank market health.

Step 02

Channel Profitability

Broke down website, warehouse, and social media channels by revenue and margin to identify where the highest-return investment should go.

Step 03

Sales Team Evaluation

Tracked all 25 sales representatives against the €100K profit target, identifying top performers and those needing support.

Step 04

Built 3 Dashboards

Created a 3-page Tableau dashboard suite covering location performance, product and channel analysis, and individual sales team tracking.

Key Findings

  • Lyon has perfect delivery (100%) but generates 65% less revenue than TurinThe issue is market penetration, not operations — a €672K gap that represents a clear recovery opportunity through better sales coverage and product-market fit.
  • The website channel delivers 62% higher margins than social mediaAt 45.8% margin vs 25.9% for social, shifting 15% of marketing budget to the website channel could generate €250K+ in additional annual profit.
  • Only 11 of 25 sales reps are hitting the €100K profit targetThe 2.2x spread between the top and bottom performer suggests territory assignments and product knowledge gaps rather than a fundamental market problem.
  • Turin, Barcelona, and Amsterdam generate 61% of total revenueThese three markets are performing well and should be defended. Zurich shows the best delivery rate (98.53%) and is ripe for volume growth.
