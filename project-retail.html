Choco de Luxe
European Sales Performance Analytics

Analysing €5.32M in luxury chocolate sales across 7 European cities to identify growth opportunities and optimise performance.

€5.32M Revenue Tableau 7 Cities 2024

The Brief

Choco de Luxe is a premium artisanal chocolate brand operating across 7 European cities through retail, online, and supermarket channels. Despite strong brand recognition, leadership was making expansion decisions without a clear picture of which cities, channels, or sales representatives were actually driving profitability.

The objective was to analyse €5.32M in revenue data across 7 cities, 3 channels, and 25 sales representatives to identify inefficiencies and build an actionable growth strategy.

What I Did

Step 01

Geographic Analysis

Compared revenue, profit, delivery success, and order volume across all 7 cities to identify top and bottom performers.

Step 02

Channel Profitability

Analysed Website vs Warehouse vs Social Media margins to find where the best return on investment actually sat.

Step 03

Sales Team Evaluation

Tracked all 25 sales representatives against the €100K profit target to identify top performers and those needing support.

Step 04

Built 3-Page Dashboard Suite

Delivered interactive Tableau dashboards covering location performance, product and channel data, and sales team rankings.

Key Findings

  • Lyon generates 65% less revenue than Turin despite perfect deliveryA €672K gap between top and bottom cities reveals a market penetration problem in Lyon — not an operational one. Reaching just 75% of Turin's performance would recover €300K+ annually.
  • Website channel delivers 62% higher margins than social mediaAt 45.8% margin vs 25.9%, the website far outperforms other channels. Shifting 15% of marketing budget to website optimisation could add €250K+ in annual profit.
  • Only 44% of sales reps are hitting their €100K target14 of 25 representatives are underperforming. The 2.2x spread between top and bottom performers suggests territory and training gaps, not market limitations.
  • 95.61% delivery success rate across all marketsOverall operations are strong. Hamburg and Brussels at 92.7% are the only cities below benchmark and represent a targeted logistics improvement opportunity.
