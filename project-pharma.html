NovaMed Solutions
Pharmaceutical Distribution Analysis

Examining £71M in distribution data across 6 countries to uncover revenue opportunities and optimise market strategy.

£71.3M Revenue Power BI 6 Countries 2024

The Brief

NovaMed Solutions distributes pharmaceutical products across six international markets, serving 200+ healthcare customers. Despite strong revenue, the leadership team lacked clear visibility into which products, regions, and customer segments were actually driving performance.

The goal was to analyse £71.3M in sales data, identify seasonal patterns and geographic opportunities, and build dashboards that would let the team make faster, more confident decisions.

What I Did

Step 01

Consolidated the Data

Cleaned and integrated 16,314 transaction records across 6 countries, creating a unified model for multi-dimensional analysis.

Step 02

Analysed Trends

Mapped revenue patterns by month, quarter, country, drug category, and customer segment to find where value was concentrated.

Step 03

Segmented Customers

Profiled the 200+ customer base by demographics, buying behaviour, and lifetime value to identify high-priority accounts.

Step 04

Built the Dashboards

Delivered two interactive Power BI dashboards — Sales Performance and Customer Analysis — with 6 live filter dimensions each.

Key Findings

  • Canada generates 44% of revenue — a concentration riskAt £31.67M, Canada dominates the portfolio. Australia (£15.25M) is the strongest growth opportunity with 2x potential in a less saturated market.
  • Top 5 drugs drive nearly a quarter of all revenueDoxycycline, Ergocalciferol, Lisinopril, Clonazepam, and Ezetimibe represent 23.3% of revenue — a classic Pareto pattern with clear stocking and promotion implications.
  • Revenue swings 32% between peak and trough monthsJune and May peak at £6.6M while March dips to £4.99M — a predictable seasonal pattern that should drive proactive inventory and staffing planning.
  • 82% profit margin held consistently across all marketsStrong, stable profitability signals a well-managed product mix. Focus should shift to volume growth in underserved markets rather than price optimisation.
