NovaMed Solutions
Pharmaceutical Distribution Analysis

Examining the full distribution pipeline to find where revenue is being lost and how the supply chain can perform better.

£71.3M Pipeline Power BI Pharmaceuticals 2024

The Brief

NovaMed Solutions is a pharmaceutical distribution company managing the movement of drugs across five product categories. Despite a large and active pipeline, the leadership team had a feeling that revenue was not being captured as efficiently as it should be.

The goal of this project was to take a close look at the entire distribution pipeline, understand where delays and losses were happening, and provide clear recommendations on how to recover revenue and strengthen operations going forward.

What I Did

Step 01

Mapped the Pipeline

Reviewed the full distribution data across all five drug categories to understand the flow of products from source to delivery.

Step 02

Identified Loss Points

Pinpointed the specific stages in the pipeline where revenue was leaking — whether through delays, compliance issues, or unfulfilled orders.

Step 03

Compared Categories

Looked at how each of the five drug categories was performing against each other to spot which areas needed the most attention.

Step 04

Built the Dashboard

Designed a Power BI dashboard giving the leadership team a real-time view of their distribution pipeline and where to focus recovery efforts.

Key Findings

  • A meaningful portion of the pipeline value was at risk of not being collected Orders that had been placed but not yet fulfilled — or had stalled at distribution — represented a recoverable revenue gap.
  • Compliance gaps were causing delays at key distribution stages Certain product categories were experiencing hold-ups due to documentation or regulatory checks that were slowing the pipeline unnecessarily.
  • Two categories were significantly underperforming relative to pipeline value Despite representing a large share of the total pipeline, these categories had the lowest conversion rates from order to delivery.
  • Targeted intervention could recover millions in outstanding revenue By addressing the identified bottlenecks and compliance issues, the company had a realistic path to recovering a significant portion of stalled revenue.
