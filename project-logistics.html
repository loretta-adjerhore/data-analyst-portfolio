SwiftRoute Logistics
Operations Optimisation Analysis

Finding the root cause of cost inefficiencies across delivery routes and building a practical plan to fix them.

78.7% Route-Cost Mismatch Tableau Logistics 2024

The Brief

SwiftRoute is a logistics company managing a large network of delivery routes. Despite reasonable volumes of deliveries going out, their operational costs were not matching what they should be for those routes — money was being spent in the wrong places and the business could not clearly see where the problems were.

The project required a detailed look at route performance and cost data to identify exactly where the inefficiencies were, then develop a structured plan for fixing them in a way that was realistic and prioritised.

What I Did

Step 01

Analysed Route Performance

Reviewed delivery data across all active routes, looking at cost, distance, time, and delivery success rates to understand where performance was breaking down.

Step 02

Identified the Mismatch

Compared expected costs against actual costs per route to find where the biggest gaps were and understand what was driving them.

Step 03

Prioritised Problem Areas

Ranked routes and operational areas by impact so the business could focus on the changes that would make the biggest difference first.

Step 04

Built a 7-Phase Roadmap

Developed a practical, phased transformation plan covering immediate quick wins through to longer-term structural improvements in the network.

Key Findings

  • The majority of routes had costs significantly misaligned with their actual output Most routes were costing more than they should relative to the deliveries they were completing, pointing to widespread inefficiency rather than isolated issues.
  • A small number of routes were responsible for a disproportionate share of excess costs Addressing the worst-performing routes first would have an outsized positive impact on overall operational efficiency.
  • Scheduling and routing decisions were a key driver of the problem Routes were not being planned in the most cost-effective way, leading to unnecessary mileage, idle time, and repeated journeys.
  • The 7-phase roadmap provides a clear, actionable path to improvement Each phase of the plan builds on the last, starting with changes that can be made immediately and progressing to deeper structural improvements over time.
