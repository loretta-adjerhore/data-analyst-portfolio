Warfarin Precision Dosing Analytics

Optimising anticoagulant therapy for 50,000 patients through data-driven risk stratification and precision dosing analysis.

50,000 Patients Power BI $20–100M Savings 2024

The Brief

GenexaHealth manages warfarin therapy for 50,000 patients across multiple care settings. Warfarin is one of the most commonly prescribed but highest-risk medications — dosing too high or too low can have serious clinical consequences.

The objective was to analyse patient data to identify risk groups, evaluate dosing patterns, and build a dashboard that would give clinical teams the visibility they need to deliver safer, more personalised care.

What I Did

Step 01

Risk Stratification

Segmented 50,000 patients into 4 risk groups based on clinical indicators, comorbidities, and dosing history to prioritise intervention.

Step 02

Dosing Pattern Analysis

Examined dosing distributions across patient segments to identify where therapy was misaligned with clinical guidelines.

Step 03

Outcome Modelling

Mapped the relationship between dosing accuracy, patient outcomes, and cost to quantify the financial impact of improved precision.

Step 04

Built the Dashboard

Delivered an interactive Power BI dashboard enabling clinical teams to filter by risk group, dosing range, and patient segment in real time.

Key Findings

  • Precision dosing could save $20–100M annuallyModelling across 50,000 patients showed that improving dosing accuracy reduces adverse events, hospital readmissions, and associated costs at significant scale.
  • High-risk patients were receiving standard-protocol dosingA segment of patients flagged as high-risk by clinical indicators were not receiving adjusted doses, representing the highest-priority intervention opportunity.
  • 4 distinct patient segments with different dosing needsSegmentation revealed that a one-size-fits-all approach was insufficient — each group required a tailored dosing strategy based on their risk profile.
  • Dashboard enables real-time clinical decision supportThe interactive filters allow clinicians to instantly surface at-risk patients by segment, reducing the time to identify candidates for dose review from days to seconds.
