GenexaHealth
Warfarin Precision Dosing Analytics

Analysing 50,000 patient records to identify why 1 in 6 patients experiences adverse events — and what data-driven dosing could do about it.

50,000 Patients Power BI Healthcare 2024

The Brief

GenexaHealth is a Utah-based precision health company serving hospitals, insurers, and pharmaceutical partners. Warfarin — one of the most widely prescribed blood thinners in the world — has a very narrow safe dosing range. Get it wrong, and patients bleed or clot. Yet most hospitals were still using a one-size-fits-all starting dose.

The project analysed 50,000 patient records to find out who was at highest risk, why standard protocols were failing them, and what a precision dosing approach could realistically save.

What I Did

Step 01

Cleaned the Data

Validated and prepared 50,000 patient records integrating genetic markers, clinical history, demographics, lifestyle factors, and drug interactions.

Step 02

Identified Risk Patterns

Analysed dose distribution and adverse event rates across patient segments to find the groups where the standard 5mg starting dose was doing the most harm.

Step 03

Stratified by Risk

Grouped patients by clinical, genetic, and lifestyle risk factors to create a clear profile of the high-risk population driving over half of all adverse events.

Step 04

Built the Dashboard

Designed an interactive Power BI dashboard with 6 filter dimensions so clinical teams can monitor patient populations and flag at-risk individuals in real time.

Key Findings

  • Nearly half of patients require doses far below the standard 5mg starting dose48.6% of patients need low doses (<3mg), meaning the standard protocol is systematically overdosing approximately 24,000 patients and creating preventable bleeding risk.
  • Three groups account for the majority of adverse eventsCKD patients (18.87% adverse rate), patients aged 76+ (16.82%), and those on Amiodarone (15.94%) drive a disproportionate share of harm despite representing just 30% of patients.
  • Smoking and heavy drinking creates a 5.5x risk multiplierPatients who are heavy drinkers and smokers experience adverse events at 5.5x the rate of non-smoking, non-drinking patients — yet lifestyle is not factored into current dosing protocols.
  • Time in Therapeutic Range is below the clinical safety goalAt 70.3% TTR vs the 75%+ target, patients are spending too long outside the safe dosing window — a systemic issue that precision dosing protocols could materially improve.
